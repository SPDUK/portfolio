# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, engineering leaders, product-minded engineers, and peers reviewing Steve P's work, writing, and current toolchain. They arrive from search, GitHub, social previews, or shared links and need a quick sense of taste, technical judgment, and credibility.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Steve P as a Cambridge-based senior frontend engineer and AI product builder. Success means visitors understand the work, trust the engineering craft, and can move naturally into projects, writing, or contact paths.

## Brand Personality

Technical, precise, energetic. The tone should feel current and hands-on without drifting into generic developer-site theatre.

## Anti-references

Avoid bland SaaS landing-page templates, generic AI-workflow dashboards, beige editorial restraint, and decorative tech visuals that do not connect to the actual portfolio.

## Design Principles

- Show the real surface: previews and supporting visuals should reflect the portfolio people are visiting.
- Keep the engineering signal clear: every section should make the stack, workflow, or output easier to understand.
- Use neon energy with discipline: the palette can glow, but hierarchy and readability come first.
- Make navigation obvious: every page should offer a direct next step into projects, writing, or home.
- Respect motion preferences: animation should add polish without hiding content or blocking reduced-motion users.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for core text and controls. Maintain keyboard access, visible focus states, reduced-motion fallbacks, and readable layouts from mobile through desktop.
